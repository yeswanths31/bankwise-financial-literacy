🛡️ Fraud & Digital Safety

Simple habits for safer digital banking and payments.

01
🔐

Never Share OTP

Keep OTPs and authentication information confidential.

02
🔢

Protect Your PIN

Keep ATM, card and UPI PINs private.

03
📱

Check UPI Requests

Verify the recipient and amount before approving a payment.

04
🎣

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not enter banking credentials into unknown links.

05
📞

Beware of Pressure Calls

Be cautious when someone creates urgency and asks for confidential data.

06
🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords where appropriate.

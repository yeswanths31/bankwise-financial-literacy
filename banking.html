LEARNING MODULE 01

🏦 Banking Basics

Build confidence with simple explanations of everyday banking.

🏦

Savings Account

A savings account is commonly used to keep money safely, make transactions and receive interest according to the account terms.

💵

Deposit

A deposit means adding money to a bank account through an available banking method.

💸

Withdrawal

A withdrawal means taking money out of an account through an approved banking channel.

🏧

ATM

ATMs can support services such as cash withdrawal and balance enquiry. Protect your PIN while using them.

📱

UPI

UPI supports digital payments and transfers. Verify the recipient and amount before approving a payment.

🏛️

RBI

The Reserve Bank of India is India's central bank and has important responsibilities relating to India's banking and financial system.
